FRAG TO FAME  /  SIMPLIFIED COAT KITS

1. Open Start_Here.png for your weapon. It shows what each mask covers on both sides.
2. Open the layered Coat_Starter.ora in an editor that supports OpenRaster, or use Coat_BaseColor.png and the black-and-white mask PNGs in your editor. Turn on individual zone layers or use a mask to select the whole zone and add your own colour. Grey-labelled stock details are left alone. Sample_Coat.png is a ready-to-preview example with all paint zones enabled.
3. Export a PNG at the original size. Which_Model.txt tells you which GLB takes the texture. These kits reuse the full kits and original files in Guns, except the M4A1, which includes a matching GLB to separate previously shared texture areas.

This simplifies editing the major parts. For designs crossing seams, keep the full kit handy and check your art on the 3D model. Magnets remain separate.
